Stock pick Picked Oct 29, 2026

SK hynix

SKHY
Price / earnings5.7×trailing
Return plan$75Bto shareholders
CapacitySold outAI memory
Why it's the pick
SK hynix makes the high-speed memory that AI chips can't run without, and it just posted a record quarter with its capacity sold out. You pay about 5.7 times last year's earnings, while many AI names trade at 30 to 100 times. It's also handing back up to $75B to shareholders. The shares slid after earnings, so you're buying record results at a lower price.
Where the risk is
Memory is a boom-and-bust business. If AI data-center spending slows, chip prices can drop fast and today's cheap earnings multiple turns out not to be cheap. Investors are already questioning how long these margins last, which is why the stock fell on good news. It's a Korean company, so currency moves and Korea-specific rules add risk.
What would change our view
Big cloud companies cutting AI budgets, or memory prices falling for two straight months.
Time frame: 1–3 yearsRisk: Medium
Crypto pick Picked Oct 29, 2026

Bitcoin

BTC
Price$78,392Oct 29
Off record high−38%peak $126,198
Market share~60%of all crypto
Why it's the pick
Bitcoin is about 38% below its record, so you're buying the biggest, most proven coin at a large discount. It fell the least of the major coins in October while most others dropped 17–30%, and its share of the crypto market climbed near 60%, a sign money is moving into it during the sell-off. Spot Bitcoin ETFs have flipped back to small inflows. It's on both Robinhood and Crypto.com, with the deepest trading of any coin.
Where the risk is
Bitcoin is down about 8% this year, and Treasury yields near 5% make safer investments more attractive. Another Fed rate hike could push it lower. The Binance hack reported Oct 30 left about $3.2B in stolen BTC and ETH that could still be sold. Bitcoin can fall 50% or more in a bad year, so only put in what you could leave alone through a big drop.
What would change our view
Bitcoin ETFs going back to steady weekly outflows, or a break below its 2026 lows.
Time frame: 2+ yearsRisk: High

How this is put together

Robinhood lists thousands of US stocks and Crypto.com lists hundreds of coins. Rather than dump every ticker, each edition ranks the most tradeable slice: S&P 500 stocks for 90-day returns, a few larger names outside it, the largest coins by market value, and the most popular meme coins.

Returns come from the dated sources listed here, so windows can differ by a few days. Robinhood carries far fewer coins than Crypto.com.
